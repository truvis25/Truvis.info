# Founder House: current design

The page answers three questions immediately: who the community is for, what members do together, and which events are coming up.

The headline is “Meet founders and investors in Abu Dhabi.” Body copy uses short, literal sentences. Calls to action say “Join the community” and “See upcoming events”. Event formats are described separately from confirmed, dated events. No dates, attendance counts, testimonials or investor relationships are invented.

The palette uses warm white (#FBFAF7), charcoal (#252420), warm grey (#655F58) and terracotta (#B64B30). Sand (#F2EEE7) separates sections and pale terracotta (#F6E7DB) indicates selected controls. DM Sans is self-hosted; Newsreader italic gives selected headings a restrained editorial accent. The existing conversation/house emblem is retained as a vector with the new palette. The header uses a readable wordmark rather than a raster image.

Upcoming events appear immediately after the hero. Loading, no events, no category matches and service failures have separate states. Each published event has a public details page and an entry in the shared calendar feed; exact venues and guest information stay in the private member area.

The form keeps two steps and clear field labels. Focus styles, labelled controls, reduced motion and mobile input sizes support keyboard and touch use. The colour test checks eight actual semantic contrast pairs; it is not a full accessibility certification.

## Research and design decisions

Founders Running Club (https://foundersrc.com/) grounds its community in shared activities. Founderss Club (https://www.founderss.club/) clearly names its audience and explains membership. Hampton (https://joinhampton.com/) explains the concrete value of membership. Startup Grind (https://www.startupgrind.com/) makes upcoming events discoverable. These inform our structure, not claims about TRUVIS members, scale or results.

Warm neutral/terracotta is a contextual creative choice. WCAG does not prescribe brand colours. Contrast and control sizing follow https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html and https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html. Important controls target at least 44px height. Statuses have text labels.

The hero is AI-generated concept photography, visibly labelled illustrative. It does not depict an actual Founder House gathering. Asset: public/assets/community-terrace.webp. Built-in imagegen prompt: five Emirati and international founders in a candid coffee conversation on a warm Abu Dhabi waterfront cafe terrace, without branded venues or specific landmark claims.
