# Founder House: current design

The page answers three questions immediately: who the community is for, what members do together, and which events are coming up. Calls to action say “Join the community” and “See upcoming events”. Event formats are described separately from confirmed, dated events. No dates, attendance counts, testimonials or investor relationships are invented.

## Approved visual reference

The owner’s October 25 `Founder House print materials.zip` supersedes the earlier four-person symbol. The approved identity now uses the nested arch/doorway mark, forest green (#07362D), turquoise (#43C6C5), the supplied lowercase wordmark and “BY TRUVIS”. The header uses the actual horizontal artwork; the footer retains its oversized live `founder house` typography. Decorative marks use the supplied green or white versions according to the background. Page headings continue in self-hosted Outfit; body copy uses DM Sans.

Forest (#07362D) is the main text and brand colour. A soft green-white (#F5F7F2) gives the page breathing room; muted green (#53675C) supports body copy. Light sage (#E2EFE8) separates sections. Turquoise (#43C6C5) accents actions, the calendar illustration, sports format and geometric details; a lighter turquoise (#69D8D1) gives the hero’s second line strong contrast against forest green. Forms retain neutral backgrounds, labelled inputs and visible keyboard focus. Dark green sections use light text (#C7D9CB). The colour test checks ten semantic contrast pairs; it is not a full accessibility certification.

The hero is now a single wide courtyard composition with an arched frame, geometric screens and a water-like turquoise threshold. It contains no photo or split card. The headline is “A home for founders. A future built together.” The next sentence names the community, founders, startup teams, investors and Abu Dhabi in plain language. The photo moves to the audience section in an arch-shaped frame. These are original vector/CSS interpretations rather than reproductions of a specific historic house or depictions of an existing clubhouse.

The motif direction draws on the shared courtyard and hospitality traditions of Abu Dhabi and Isfahan: an arcade/entry frame, restrained eight-point geometric rosettes, lattice/sash-like openings and a central water-like shape. The Cultural Foundation’s own account describes courtyards, arched transitions, star-shaped tile/wood patterns and water (https://culturalfoundation.ae/en/the-courtyard). Visit Iran describes the courtyard, porch, pond, woodwork and muqarnas of Sukias House in Isfahan (https://visitiran.ir/en/attraction/Sukias-House). The Philadelphia Museum of Art’s actual Safavid domestic room documents geometric and floral stucco in a residential complex near Isfahan (https://www.philamuseum.org/objects/44116). These sources ground the visual interpretation; we do not claim that our pattern is an exact historic regional design. Assets: `public/assets/courtyard.svg` and a separately composed narrow-screen `courtyard-mobile.svg`.

Page headings use large rounded Outfit; desktop hero type scales to 105px, section headings to 58px. Main body copy is 18–21px; mobile body text is generally 17–18px, with 42px section headings and 45–65px hero type. Supporting UI and captions use a readable 12–16px scale. Forms, FAQs and member/admin screens also receive the larger reading scale. Important touch controls aim for at least 44px height. The layout reflows for phones and respects reduced-motion settings.

Upcoming events appear immediately after the hero. Loading, no events, no category matches and service failures have separate states. Each published event has a public details page and an entry in the shared calendar feed; exact venues and guest information stay in the private member area. The application keeps two steps and clear labels.

## Research and design decisions

Founders Running Club (https://foundersrc.com/) grounds its community in shared activities. Founderss Club (https://www.founderss.club/) clearly names its audience and explains membership. Hampton (https://joinhampton.com/) explains the concrete value of membership. Startup Grind (https://www.startupgrind.com/) makes upcoming events discoverable. These inform our structure, not claims about TRUVIS members, scale or results.

The user-supplied green identity supersedes the earlier warm neutral/terracotta direction. WCAG does not prescribe brand colours. Contrast and control sizing follow https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html and https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html. Statuses also have text labels.

The audience-section photo is AI-generated concept photography, visibly labelled illustrative. It does not depict an actual Founder House gathering. Asset: public/assets/community-terrace.webp. Original image-generation brief: five Emirati and international founders in a candid coffee conversation on an Abu Dhabi waterfront cafe terrace, without branded venues or specific landmark claims.

## Footer and brand finishing

The footer follows the user's Google Labs reference (https://labs.google/, visually reviewed October 24, 2026): an open canvas, large abstract forms, a very large brand name and restrained horizontal link rows. Its own shapes use the existing green/turquoise identity: an entrance arch, an eight-point rosette, the supplied arch logo symbol and a lattice window. The owner explicitly retained the oversized, lowercase `founder house` text across the footer; keep it as live Outfit typography with the original responsive sizing, rather than replacing it with the horizontal logo image. The new arch mark belongs in the decorative row and the supplied horizontal logo remains in the header.

The footer credits “© 2026 truvis.ae. All rights reserved.” and links to https://truvis.ae/. Written website addresses use lowercase, including truvis.info. The owner explicitly selected info@truvis.info as the footer email; it replaces the earlier corporate email. The office address and fixed telephone remain Level 36, Tower 3, Etihad Towers, Abu Dhabi, UAE; +971 2 409 3184. Source for the address/telephone: https://truvis.ae/contact-us/. No company mobile or WhatsApp contact is included. The address is labelled as TRUVIS International Services, rather than an event venue.

`public/assets/brand/` contains size derivatives of the supplied PNG artwork, preserving its proportions, colours and clear space. Header sources are 600/1200px wide WebP; the 1831px derivative is available for other large brand placements. The footer wordmark uses live text. Decorative green/white marks use 128/256/512px sources. The supplied 32px favicon, 180px Apple touch icon and 192/512px app icons are copied unchanged; `app/favicon.ico` adds a browser fallback. The home-screen manifest and 1200×630 sharing card use the new identity. Transactional emails use a 600px PNG displayed at 230px for email-client compatibility. No supplied logo is recoloured or rotated. The new asset URLs refresh the browser’s old logo references.
